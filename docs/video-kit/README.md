## The three promises
The hackathon asks "Would you let AI shop for you?" People say no out of three fears: it'll get tricked, it'll overspend,
I won't know what it did. The four beats show the answers. Say them over the beats, in these words:

1. **Goose can't be fooled into paying.** Goose can't pay. Only your tap in PayPal does. The Watcher can miss a scam, so you see the exact cart first.
2. **Goose can't overspend.** Your limits live in code, where a seller's words can't change them. Code checks each cart before your tap and again before money moves.
3. **Goose can't hide anything.** Every decision in your visit is a row with its reason. Rows are only added, never changed.

The line that says where it fits: **PayPal protects the payment; Goose protects everything before it.**
The line to remember: **Nothing is paid until you tap.**
