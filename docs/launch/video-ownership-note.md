# The video ownership note (draft, for Joshua)

For Joshua (his 5:40 PM message, Thu Nov 5 2026). Drafted by Claude. This is a plain-language note, not legal advice.

**Why it's needed.** The Official Rules, read live on Thu Nov 5: "An Entrant may contract with a third party for
technical assistance to create the Submission provided the Submission components are solely the Entrant's work product
and the result of the Entrant's ideas and creativity, and the Entrant owns all rights to them." Your friend editing the
video is that third-party help. The note puts in writing that the video is yours.

**How to use it.** Send the note below to your friend. They fill in their name and the date and send it back by email
or text (a reply that says "I agree" with the note quoted is enough for a written record). Keep it until the winners are
announced on Dec 21. Don't commit their reply to the repo: it has their name and contact details.

---

> and Lucas are entering in the PayPal AI hackathon on Devpost.
>
> 1. I'm giving technical help (editing, and any voice, music or graphics work I do) at Joshua's direction. The ideas,
>    the script and the story are his and his team's.
> 2. The video, and every edit, file, recording and draft I make for it, belongs to Joshua Javier. I give him all rights
>    I have in them, and I won't claim any rights in the video.
> 3. Joshua may publish the video on YouTube, submit it to the hackathon, and use it after the hackathon.
> 4. I won't add anything I don't have the right to use: no music, footage, images or brand names from anyone else
>    without permission. If I use an AI voice or an editing tool, its terms allow this use.
> 5. I'm not a member of the Gilded Goose team and I'm not entering the hackathon. I won't receive any part of a prize.
